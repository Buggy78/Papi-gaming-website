🏆 GAMING TIPS

🎯 Improve Your Aim

Practise target tracking and switching between targets. Adjust sensitivity gradually instead of changing everything at once.

🏃 Improve Movement

Practise sliding, jumping and using cover. Build consistent movement habits before increasing speed.

🪂 Battle Royale

Choose your landing area carefully, collect equipment, watch the safe zone and avoid unnecessary fights.

🎮 Better Performance

Close unnecessary background apps, keep your device cool and select graphics settings that maintain stable gameplay.
